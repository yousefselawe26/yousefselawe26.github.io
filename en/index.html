CleanSpace

Photo & storage cleaner

CleanSpace: Clean up your iPhone storage in one tap.

It gathers duplicates, look-alikes, blurry shots, screenshots and oversized videos in one place, so you can clear them in seconds. Every scan runs on your phone — not one photo is uploaded.

  • Eight kinds of clutter: duplicates, look-alikes, bursts, blurry shots and more.
  • Swipe mode: right to keep, left to delete, one photo at a time.
  • A Secret Vault, encrypted and opened with Face ID or a passcode.
