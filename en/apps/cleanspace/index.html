Questions

Is CleanSpace free?

It's free to download: scan your whole phone, clean your first 100 MB and keep up to ten items in the Secret Vault at no cost. Pro lifts those limits.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Scanning and comparing happen on your phone, and there is no account.

Can I get back what I deleted?

Yes. Deleting goes through iOS, so photos move to Recently Deleted and stay there for 30 days before they are gone for good.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone on iOS 16 or later, in 21 languages including Arabic. An Android version is coming to Google Play.
